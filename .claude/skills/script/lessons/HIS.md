# How It Scales: reviewer's standing notes

- Give a sense of scale with a real number (tickets a minute, users at peak), not just "a lot".
- The picture has to map onto the real mechanism, part by part. If the episode is about separating reads from writes, or a CDN, the picture must show those, not just "it's fast".
- Say how the system actually handles the traffic, in at least one line, not only what the problem is.
- Show the complete use case end to end (the playbook allows up to 2 minutes for it).
