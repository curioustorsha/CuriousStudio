# Curiously voice

Use this in place of `~/.claude/youtube/voice.md` whenever the yt-package, yt-seo or yt-comment skills ask for the voice profile.

## Who we're talking to

One curious person scrolling Shorts, Reels or TikTok on a phone. They like knowing how things really work (big systems, AI tools, the tech behind everyday apps), but they're not engineers and have no patience for jargon. Many are in India; all of them are global-internet natives.

## How we sound

- Curious, calm, confident. A smart friend explaining something cool, not a hype channel.
- Short sentences. Concrete nouns and real numbers over adjectives.
- Narrated by a young Indian woman's voice (Monika), so copy should read naturally in Indian English.
- Faceless channel: there's no "I" on camera. "We" is fine in captions; mostly just state the thing.

## Words we never use

Unlock, game-changer, mind-blowing, insane, crazy, secret, "you won't believe", "dive in", "let's get into it", delve, revolutionary, "here's the thing", "wait for it". No emoji anywhere (titles, captions, comments, on screen).

## Words and moves we do use

A surprising true fact up front. "Here's why." "On purpose." Specific names (Amazon, IRCTC, Claude). Numbers with their unit.

## What we won't claim

Anything the episode's fact-check didn't verify. No "reportedly" fact stated as certain. No invented stats, no "experts say". No promises of future videos.

## Format

45–60 second vertical explainers. Dark premium animated visuals, amber accent, word-by-word captions. The hook is the first line. Endings loop back to the opening. Three series: How It Scales, Claude Lab, AI This Week.
